About Me

Hi there! I'm a Master's student in Computer Science at the University of Southern California, graduating in May 2027. Before USC, I earned my B.Eng. in AI & Software from Gachon University, Korea, in 2025. These days I'm fascinated by robot learning, especially world models, simulation for evaluating robot policies, and vision-language-action (VLA) models.

I'm currently applying for PhD positions starting in Fall 2027, and I'm always happy to connect.
